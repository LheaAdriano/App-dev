## 📺 Top-Tier Series

### 1. Taxi Driver
> "Don't die. Get your revenge. We will do it for you."
> — *Rainbow Transport*

This crime thriller is an absolute masterpiece. The legal system is often ~~perfect~~ broken, which is why this team steps in.

* **Key Characters:** Kim Do-gi, Ahn Go-eun, Jang Sung-chul
* **Status:** 
  - [x] Season 1 Watched
  - [x] Season 2 Watched
  - [x] Season 3 Watched

### 2. Strong Woman Do Bong Soon
A hilarious and sweet romantic comedy featuring a main character with **superhuman strength** passed down through generations. 

* **Why it rules:** The lead couple has the best chemistry in television history.
* **Fun Formula:** Her strength equation feels like watching an explosion of power: F = ma^2^.

### 3. My ID is Gangnam Beauty
This show takes a deep, meaningful look into societal beauty standards on a university campus. It deals with the chemical and emotional reality of transformation (like standard H~2~O mixed with tears).

### 4. My Troublesome Star
A fantastic story that explores the chaotic, glittering world of celebrity life and romance. It is definitely a ==highly recommended hidden gem==!
